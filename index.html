I’m a postdoctoral researcher in the TEA Group at the University of British Columbia, working with Xiaoxiao Li on mechanistic interpretability for vision–language models (VLMs). I use information theory to investigate how these models represent visual information and which internal computations use that information to produce an answer.

I began my Ph.D. in Electronic Science and Technology at Xi’an Jiaotong University in 2018 with Feng Liang. During my doctorate, I did joint research at Simon Fraser University with Jie Liang. I completed my Ph.D. in 2023 with a dissertation on end-to-end image compression using autoencoders.

After graduating, I worked with Jie Liang and Zhenman Fang at SFU on data compression and human activity recognition. My earlier compression research explored wavelet transforms and entropy models for learned image and video codecs. I joined UBC in September 2025.

ChWDTA: Channel-wise Wavelet-Domain Transformer Attention and Entropy Modeling for Learned Image Compression

Haisheng Fu, Runyu Yang, Feng Ding, Siyu Zhu, Jie Liang, Xiaoxiao Li, Zhenman Fang, Jingning Han

IEEE Transactions on Image Processing · Accepted, 2026

We apply wavelet transforms across feature channels inside windowed attention and before entropy coding. The eight-slice model saves 17.82% on Kodak, 19.15% on CLIC Professional Validation, and 22.56% on Tecnick in BD-rate versus VVC-Intra (VTM 9.1).

  • Preprint
  • Code
  • Original figures
Method and runtime

The attention block computes query, key, and value projections after the channel transform. A two-level wavelet packet splits latents into four subbands for entropy coding. Using one slice per subband still saves 17.24% on Kodak and takes 187 ms to encode and decode, compared with 225 ms for eight slices on an RTX 4090 at 768 × 512 (batch size 1).

VLMProbe: An Open-source Library for VLM Probing and Interpretation

Gexin Huang, Haisheng Fu, Yanting Yang, Chen Zhao, Ruinan Jin, Jiaqi Liu, Xiaoxiao Li

arXiv · 2026

VLMProbe brings lens readouts, attention attribution, causal interventions, and sparse autoencoder analysis into one interface. In our study, attribution maps sometimes disagreed with measured intervention effects, showing why it helps to test explanations with more than one method.

FEDS: Feature and Entropy-Based Distillation Strategy for Efficient Learned Image Compression

Haisheng Fu, Jie Liang, Feng Liang, Guohe Zhang, Jingning Han

Pattern Recognition · 2027 issue

Uses a high-performing teacher to train a smaller codec through output supervision, feature alignment, and entropy-guided selection of latent channels.

  • Publisher
Reported results

The compact student has 63% fewer parameters and about 73% lower encoding and decoding time than its teacher. The reported BD-rate increases are 1.24% on Kodak, 1.17% on Tecnick, and 0.55% on CLIC.

SELIC: Semantic-Enhanced Learned Image Compression via High-Level Textual Guidance

Haisheng Fu, Jie Liang, Zhenman Fang, Jingning Han

IEEE ICME · 2025 · Oral

Uses textual semantics derived from the input image to guide its compressed representation. The decoder reconstructs the image directly from the bitstream.

  • Publisher
  • Preprint
Reported results

The paper reports a 0.10–0.15 dB PSNR gain over the nonsemantic baseline and 4.9% BD-rate savings relative to VVC.

3DM-WeConvene: Learned Image Compression with 3D Multi-Level Wavelet-Domain Convolution and Entropy Model

Haisheng Fu, Jie Liang, Feng Liang, Zhenman Fang, Guohe Zhang, Jingning Han

arXiv · 2025

Extends the wavelet transform across space and channels, applies subband-specific convolution, and codes low-frequency components first to provide context.

  • Preprint
Reported results

The preprint reports BD-rate savings of 12.24% on Kodak, 15.51% on Tecnick 100, and 12.97% on CLIC relative to H.266/VVC.

WeConvene: Learned Image Compression with Wavelet-Domain Convolution and Entropy Model

Haisheng Fu, Jie Liang, Zhenman Fang, Jingning Han, Feng Liang, Guohe Zhang

ECCV · 2024

Brings discrete wavelet transforms into a CNN codec and its entropy model. The coder uses low-frequency coefficients to predict distributions for the high-frequency bands.

  • Publisher
  • Code
  • Author manuscript
Reported results

On Kodak, adding Haar wavelet-domain convolution improves BD-rate savings relative to H.266/VVC from 0.4% to 4.7%. Wavelet-domain entropy modeling brings the saving to 8.2%.

Haisheng Fu, Feng Liang, Jie Liang, Yongqiang Wang, Zhenman Fang, Guohe Zhang, Jingning Han

IEEE TIP · 2024

Combines a parallel checkerboard context model, deformable residual blocks, and decoder distillation to reduce the cost of learned image coding.

  • Publisher

Reported results

In the authors’ comparison with GLLMM, the proposed method encoded about 20× faster and decoded 70–90× faster, with a 2.3% BD-rate saving.
